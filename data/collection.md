## Preface

Headline: *It starts with one* — one record, then another, then a lifetime of them.

Hello, whoever reads my blog. My name is Dymitr and I'm a music passionate from Poland. I live in Poznań, where my vinyl story began. My relationship with music started much earlier though — as a child with a cassette player, then a student with an MP3 player, and then streaming showed up. And I found myself a little frustrated with it. You don't like a track — you swipe left, then left again, and before you know it you've spent ten minutes swiping instead of actually listening. Sometimes you only catch that one song you already knew. But some tracks need time — the same way wine does — before they really open up for you.
Vinyl showed me that. This ancient technology has a way of making you slow down. It's not just putting something on in the background — there's a whole little ritual to it: you place the record on the platter, get the motor going, run an antistatic brush over the grooves, and then you get that warm, crackly pop as the stylus touches down. And unlike a file sitting on a server somewhere, you can pick this thing up. It's yours in a way a Spotify stream never quite is. You also can't really skip the tracks you're not sure about — well, you can, but it feels wrong — so you end up listening to a whole side, and sometimes the track you nearly skipped turns out to be your favorite.
I don't think there are bad bands or singers, just music that's not for you. I'm mostly a rock person, but I'll follow a good song anywhere — jazz, pop, hip-hop, whatever moves me. I also like buying records without previewing them first. That first listen, completely cold, on vinyl — it's worth keeping special. Most of my records come from online shops, though I do make it to local stores occasionally, and whenever I travel abroad I try to bring a record home from wherever I've been.
I've started noticing that records connect to each other, and to moments, and to things happening out in the world. That's what this site is about.

Signoff: So — here we go. A journey about to begin…

### 01. Linkin Park — Papercuts

<!-- release:30348842 master:3453923 -->
2024-08-06

From: Internet, Poland

Sparked By: Previnyl

It starts with Linkin Park. One of my favorite bands, ever since I first heard "In The End." They're the kind of band you fall for on the first song. Each new album from Minutes to Midnight always sounded like a new band — but the band you loved from the very first listen. Their experiments with different styles always felt awesome. And everything changes — the world changes, you change, the band and their music change — but you still love it. Isn't that something? This compilation album was almost predefined as the first item in my collection.
